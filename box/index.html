Novaforge Studio

Paste a script, choose what to make, press Start.
One-time setup needed

No GitHub token found. In Termux run:

read -rsp "Paste token: " T; echo; printf 'GITHUB_TOKEN=%s\n' "$T" > ~/.novaforge-studio.env; chmod 600 ~/.novaforge-studio.env; unset T

Then stop the box (Ctrl+C) and start it again.

Script format 
---
title: Your video title
brand: Vouchr
handle: @yourhandle
cta: Link in bio
---
## First point
One or two short sentences, spoken and shown.

## Second point
Next section...

### Caption
#tags for the post
Brand name, handle and closing line in the boxes above override the same lines in your script. Leave them empty to use the script's own header. ChatGPT-style slides also work: ## Slide 2 followed by **Heading** - text. A heading with no text is spoken instead. Keep each scene to 1-3 sentences.
