1. Give the pictures

Body parts: draw a box around each part picture, then say which part and which view it is. Names become part__view (for example upper_arm_l__left_profile). Draw each part once: the other side and the mirror views are filled in by flipping, and the grid at the bottom shows what is still missing.
How this learns (honest version)

There is no neural network here. The page traces each picture into vector shapes with its own tracer, rasterises the vector again and measures how close it is to your picture. Needs refinement and Different re-run the tracer with other settings and keep the closest result. Accurate saves the vector and the settings, and the next picture in the same category and style starts from the settings you approved (settings you marked Different are avoided). Nothing is sent anywhere: all work happens in this browser.
