# Why every district needs its own network

## Local problems need local connections
Most platforms are built for big cities. Small districts get left out, even though the people there know exactly what is needed.

## Trust travels faster close to home
When people can find and vouch for each other nearby, work gets done faster. A recommendation from a neighbour beats a hundred anonymous reviews.

## Start small, then connect
Begin with one district, learn what people actually use, and then link the next one. Growth that follows real needs lasts longer.
